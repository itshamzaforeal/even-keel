The approach

Consistency, not intensity.

Most fitness marketing sells intensity — the workout that wrecks you, the program that promises transformation in six weeks. We've watched that model burn out exactly the people it claims to help. Even Keel is built on a different premise: the training that works is the training you can actually keep doing. Twenty steady weeks beat two brutal ones, every time.

We start with your actual week, not an idealized one. If you have forty minutes before school pickup, a shoulder that's been cranky since you moved apartments, or a training history that stalled three years ago, that's the starting point — not an obstacle to work around. Programming adapts to your schedule, your sleep, your stress load, and your body on any given week, because a plan that ignores real life doesn't survive contact with it.

If your last experience with fitness left you sore, discouraged, or quietly convinced you'd failed at it, you're exactly who we built this for. There's no leaderboard here, no shame around modifying, no coach implying you should be doing more. We measure progress in consistency, capability, and how your body feels doing the things your life actually requires — carrying a toddler, sitting through a long flight, climbing stairs without thinking about it.

How it works

Four steps, in order, from first call to steady rhythm.

01

Intro call & goals

A 30-minute conversation about your history, your week, and what "better" would actually look like for you.

02

Movement check-in

A relaxed, no-pressure look at how you move today — mobility, strength baselines, anything that needs care.

03

Weekly sessions, your schedule

Recurring sessions slotted into the hours you actually have, in person around Portland or by video.

04

Monthly check-in & adjust

We revisit the plan every four weeks and adjust for what's changed — energy, schedule, goals, life.
